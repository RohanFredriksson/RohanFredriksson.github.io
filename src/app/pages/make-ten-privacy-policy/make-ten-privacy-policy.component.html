Make Ten Privacy Policy


RojuanoDev built the Make Ten app as a free app, supported by advertising. This service is provided by RojuanoDev at no cost and is intended for use as is.


This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our service.


Information We Collect


Make Ten has no account or sign-in, and we never ask you for your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts.


To serve and measure ads, our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS or advertising ID on Android) and advertising data, such as which ads were shown to you and how you interacted with them. This identifier is linked to your device and is used for tracking, including to show you more relevant ads. See the Advertising section below for how to opt out.


Information Stored on Your Device


Your puzzle preferences (such as the spoiler guard, exponentials rule, and appearance settings) and a small amount of local app state are stored only on your device, using your device's local storage. This information is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is removed automatically when you delete the app.


Camera and Scanning


Make Ten uses your device's camera only to read the 4-digit number on a train carriage. Camera frames are analysed entirely on your device by the app's built-in text recognition, and are never stored, saved to your photo library, or uploaded anywhere.


The first time you use the scanner, the app downloads the text-recognition engine and English language data it needs from a content delivery network (jsDelivr). Like any web request, this exposes your device's IP address to that provider. No image or scan data is included in this request.


Advertising


Make Ten shows banner and occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. Where permitted, these ads may be personalised using your device's advertising identifier. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services at google.com/policies/privacy/partners .


On iOS, Make Ten asks for your permission before using your advertising identifier, in line with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can opt out of personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.


In-App Purchases


Make Ten may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see or store your payment card or bank details. Purchase entitlements are managed for us by RevenueCat, which assigns your device an anonymous app user identifier that is not tied to your real-world identity, solely to remember that you've purchased ad removal.


Log Data


We do not run our own servers and do not collect any log data from the app ourselves. The third-party services described above may collect their own technical and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies.


Cookies


Make Ten itself does not use cookies. Our advertising partner may use device identifiers and similar local storage technologies as part of serving and measuring ads.


Service Providers


We work with the following third-party service providers to operate Make Ten:


Google AdMob — serves and measures the ads shown in the app, and may collect your advertising identifier and advertising data. See policies.google.com/privacy .


RevenueCat — manages in-app purchase entitlements using an anonymous app user identifier. See revenuecat.com/privacy .


jsDelivr — a content delivery network used to download the on-device text-recognition engine and language data described above. See jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net .


Security


We collect the minimum data necessary to run Make Ten and serve ads, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. Your privacy and security are of utmost importance to us. However, please keep in mind that the security of the device on which the app is installed is the responsibility of the user. We recommend keeping your device updated with the latest security patches and using appropriate security measures to protect your personal information.


Links to Other Sites


Ads shown in Make Ten may link to third-party advertiser websites or apps. We do not control and are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these third parties, and we encourage you to review their own privacy policies.


Children’s Privacy


Make Ten is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.


Your Choices


You can opt out of personalised advertising or reset your device's advertising identifier at any time in your device settings, revoke the app's camera permission if you no longer wish to use the scanner, or delete the app to remove all locally stored settings.


Changes to This Privacy Policy


We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
